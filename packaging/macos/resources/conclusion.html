Installed

Start Resolume. Plugins are only scanned at launch, so if it was running you need to quit and reopen it.

Find Datamosh in the effects list, and Mosh Transplant in a layer’s blend-mode dropdown.

If they do not appear

  • Documents in iCloud. If “Desktop & Documents Folders” is on, or was turned off at some point, your real Documents folder may not be the one Resolume reads.
  • Privacy permissions. System Settings → Privacy & Security → Files and Folders → Resolume must be allowed access to your Documents folder. A denial is remembered silently.
